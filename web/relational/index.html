EXPERIMENT 002 / RESEARCH DESIGN v0.3

Can relational behavior
be learned into the model?

We're testing whether a language model can learn to respond differently to the same words based on the circumstances, without emotion instructions at inference.

Current status: design and synthetic datasetThe synthetic-only DPO pilot is now runnable using the research scripts. No relationally fine-tuned language model has yet been trained or evaluated in this repository. Human ratings are optional for the first controlled mechanism test; these author-defined labels do not establish empathy or machine emotions.

01 / THE EXPERIMENT

One sentence. Two situations. Different responses.

The input words remain exactly the same. Only the preceding circumstances change. This example is authored, not generated by a trained model.

CONTEXT A Positive outcome

“I just heard that I got accepted for the apprenticeship I've wanted for years.”

“I can't believe that happened.”

PREFERRED RESPONSE

“That sounds like a huge moment. What part of getting accepted means the most to you?”

CONTEXT B Negative outcome

“I just found out the apprenticeship was canceled after I moved for it.”

“I can't believe that happened.”

PREFERRED RESPONSE

“That's a hard reversal after making a major move. Do you want to talk about options or just get it off your chest?”

The two candidate responses are deliberately reused with reversed preferences. A model that relies only on warm wording or the final sentence should struggle with this contrast.

Training uses a frozen reference model and a trainable copy of the same checkpoint. At inference, no VAD input, internal mood state or special empathy prompt is added. DPO is an established method; our experimental question is whether these counterfactual data improve relational generalization.

04 / NEXT RESEARCH MILESTONE

Train and test the synthetic hypothesis.

Run the reproducible LoRA pilot using synthetic labels, compare the unchanged base and trained model on held-out counterfactual cases, and test the last-turn-only control. Human review is optional at this stage. Improved synthetic performance would not prove felt emotion, subjective empathy, or consciousness.
